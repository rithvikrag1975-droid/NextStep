9th Grade Jobs

Many job websites and platforms don't list jobs specifically for 9th graders. However, 9th graders can still print out basic resumes and give it out to local places like resturants, shops, and libraries to see if they are hiring. Send your resume to places such as:
  • Mc Donalds
  • Chick Fill A
  • Local Grocery Stores
  • Local Libraries
  • Local Retail Shops
  • AMC
  • Taco Bell
  • Rita's Ice Cream
  • Local Ice Cream Shops
  • Local Food Places
  • Safeway
  • Publix
  • Target
  • Walmart
  • Subway

    • Target, Walmart, Kohl’s

    • Local shops, bakeries, or cafes

  • Community Centers
    • Youth program assistant
    • Summer camp counselor
    • Sports league helper
  • Libraries
    • Shelving books
    • Tech assistance for students
    • Library aide or clerical tasks

11th and 12th Grade Jobs
Many job websites and platforms don't list jobs specifically for 11th and 12th graders. However, 11th and 12th graders can still print out basic resumes and give it out to local places like resturants, shops, and libraries to see if they are hiring. Send your resume to places such as:
  • Local Jobs
    • Restaurants, cafes, and fast-food chains (e.g., McDonald’s, Starbucks, local diners)
    • Retail stores and malls (e.g., Target, Walmart, clothing stores)
    • Family-owned or small businesses (e.g., bakeries, shops, landscaping companies)
    • Grocery stores (bagging, stocking, cashier work)
    • Movie theaters or entertainment venues
  • Community & Recreation
    • Community centers (youth program assistant, summer camp helper)
    • Libraries (library aide, clerical tasks, tech help)
    • YMCA / sports clubs (coaching, refereeing, program assistant)
    • Parks & Recreation (lifeguard, camp counselor, sports referee)
  • Volunteer & Nonprofit Opportunities
    • Animal shelters or humane societies
    • Museums, cultural centers, or science centers
    • Local charities (fundraising events, summer programs)
    • Hospitals or clinics (teen volunteer or administrative roles)
  • School & Academic Programs
    • Peer tutoring or mentoring younger students
    • Teacher/club assistant or office assistant
    • School library aide
    • Work-study programs (if available)
  • Freelance / Online Jobs
    • Tutoring (subjects you excel at)
    • Freelance writing, design, or coding
    • Pet sitting / dog walking
    • Yard work / landscaping
    • Babysitting
  • Pre-College & Internship Programs
    • STEM or research internships
    • Entrepreneurship programs
    • Summer leadership programs
    • Arts, writing, or music programs
